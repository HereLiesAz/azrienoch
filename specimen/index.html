Named instances

Azrienoch doesn't draw its own letterforms. It extracts Roboto Flex's outlines, spacing and kerning at chosen points in its own variable design space, then adds two things: a wght axis correlated with cap-height and x-height so a heavier instance is a genuinely taller one, and a SERF axis that grows slab feet from nothing, on stems Roboto Flex already ends flat.

Counter width is pushed wide at every weight, most pointedly at Black — refusing the usual trade where ink crowds out the void.

Display

Height is a matter of weight, not size.

Text

Roboto Flex keeps weight and height independent on purpose — that is the entire premise of exposing eight parametric axes separately from wght and wdth. Azrienoch does the opposite: its weight axis is mapped onto a correlated path through that same space, so cap-height, x-height and stroke weight all grow together. A spurred G and a kicked R leg are barely-noticeable nods to Akzidenz-Grotesk, sitting on Roboto Flex's own analytical, Helvetica-adjacent rationalism.
