Named instances

Azrienoch doesn't draw its own letterforms from scratch. It takes Jost's own outlines (a real, professionally drawn geometric sans) as its starting point, samples them across wght (100-900) and a per-glyph ink-density weighted compression for wdth, then adds a SERF axis that grows slab feet from nothing on stems Jost already ends flat, and a GRAD axis (approximated by sampling a nearby weight while holding the advance width fixed) for optical compensation that changes stroke weight without reflowing a line of text.

'c'/'e' are cut directly from this font's own 'o' (a ring, sliced open) so their bowls always match its proportions exactly; 's' reads as Helvetica-derived, sourced from Arimo; 'a' is single-story, built from 'd's own outline rather than the double-story convention most grotesques inherit from print.

Display

Height is a matter of weight, not size.

Text

Jost's own cap-height and x-height stay fixed across its entire weight range on purpose, so a heavy and a light line share the same baseline geometry — mixed-weight multi-line layouts align without per-weight size compensation. Azrienoch keeps that invariant and adds a slab foot on top: a stem that ends flat in the sans default grows a same-wound rectangle at SERF>0, collapsed to a hairline at SERF=0, so the whole Thin-to-Black, Normal-to-Condensed, Sans-to-Slab range compiles as one continuously interpolating variable font.
